# ⚡ WattExchange  

🏆 **2nd Place – Hack the Sem 2026**  

---

## 🌱 About the Project  
WattExchange is a peer-to-peer solar energy trading platform that uses AI-based matching and Solana settlements to let households buy and sell surplus solar energy directly with their neighbours.  

---

## 🚀 Inspiration  
In many countries like Australia, households with solar panels generate excess energy but are underpaid when selling it back to the grid. At the same time, nearby households pay high retail prices for the same electricity. We wanted to bridge this gap by enabling direct energy exchange within communities.  

---

## 🎯 What It Solves  
- Fair compensation for households generating surplus solar energy  
- Lower electricity costs for buyers  
- More efficient use of renewable energy within neighborhoods  
- Reduced reliance on centralized energy grids  

---

---

## ⚙️ Key Features  
- **P2P Energy Marketplace** – Buy and sell surplus energy locally  
- **Geolocation Matching** – Connect with nearby households  
- **AI-Based Matching** – Smart ranking based on price and proximity  
- **Real-Time Updates** – Dynamic listings and trade updates  
- **Secure Transactions** – Blockchain-backed payments using Solana  
- **Energy Simulation** – Realistic, formula-driven energy data
